## Current Patch102 transport assessment

`http_request_limited()` closes the HTTP transport after any failed `esp_http_client_perform()`, logs the close result, and preserves the client handle. In the verified ESP-IDF v6.0.1 source, successful `esp_http_client_close()` sets the handle state to `HTTP_STATE_INIT` and closes its transport; the next ordinary `esp_http_client_perform()` enters its INIT/connect path. This is already the recovery behavior for a failed request and does not add a logical inventory request. The supplied diagnostics do not establish that the close failed; the later successful sequence-43 observation also provides no evidence of a persistent recovery defect. Keep Patch102 transport unchanged and observe longer before proposing a transport change.
